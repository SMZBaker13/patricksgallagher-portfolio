Culver City · Accessory dwelling unit

From unpermitted unit to legal ADU

An existing backyard dwelling had never been permitted. I managed it through architecture, plan submission, permitting, a full renovation and inspections until the city approved it as a legal accessory dwelling unit.

The starting point

A detached dwelling unit that was in use but had never been permitted. Before it could be renovated, it needed drawings, a formal plan submission and city approval, followed by construction to current code.

What I managed

  • Engaged and coordinated the architect through blueprint development
  • Prepared and submitted plans to the municipal building department and worked directly with city staff
  • Ran the legalization process alongside code-compliance requirements
  • Selected, hired and coordinated contractors across multiple disciplines
  • Handled procurement, scheduling and sequencing through construction
  • Scheduled inspections and coordinated corrections through final approval
