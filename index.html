APPROACH

How I run a job, start to finish

  1. PHASE 1

    Scope & budget

    Scope development, cost estimates, project budgets, and soliciting and evaluating subcontractor bids.

  2. PHASE 2

    Design & permitting

    Hiring architects, plan review, municipal submissions, energy-efficiency documentation and working directly with city staff.

  3. PHASE 3

    Construction

    Trade scheduling and sequencing, procurement and deliveries, change management and hands-on field work.

  4. PHASE 4

    Inspection & closeout

    Scheduling inspections, resolving inspector comments and coordinating corrections through completion.
