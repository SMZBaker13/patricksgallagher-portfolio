Santa Monica · 3 bd / 2.5 ba townhouse

Townhouse interior renovation

A full interior redo of a two-story townhouse. The kitchen and bathrooms came down to the studs, the old terracotta tile came up, and a wet bar became a built-in pantry. Behind the walls, the water heater and sewer line were replaced.

What I managed

  • Set the scope and budget for the kitchen, baths, floors and storage
  • Handled plans and permits
  • Bid out and hired the trades, then scheduled them room by room
  • Ordered cabinets, tile and fixtures and tracked deliveries
  • Booked inspections and closed out corrections
